Circular icon-only control — topbar actions, card overflow menus, composer tools, marketing icon tiles.

```jsx
<IconButton icon="settings" variant="plain" label="Settings" />
<IconButton icon="plus" variant="onAccent" size="lg" label="Add account" />
```

Always a perfect circle. `tile` is the grey-gradient circle from the reference's icon grid;
`plain` is the quiet topbar variant; `onAccent` is the white circle used on the green hero card.
`active` gives it the accent-faint selected treatment.

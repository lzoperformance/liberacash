The assistant's empty state — and the system's only animated-feeling element, the blurred orb
(`--gradient-ai-orb`), which stands in for an avatar.

```jsx
<AIPromptPanel
  heading="How Can I Assist You With Your Finances?"
  subheading="Quickly track cash flow, get AI-powered insights, and manage your money—all in one place"
  suggestions={['Show me my cash flow','Help me set a savings goal','Forecast my balance','Plan my monthly budget']}
  model="Choose Model" />
```

Headings are questions. Use `compact` when it sits inside a dashboard card beside other modules.
